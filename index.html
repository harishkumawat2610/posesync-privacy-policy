PPoseSync AI Camera

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

PoseSync AI Camera is designed to process pose information on your device. Please read this policy to understand how app permissions, saved photos, and advertising services are handled.

1. Introduction

PoseSync AI Camera ("we", "our", or "the app") is a pose-matching camera application that helps users recreate poses, capture photographs, and compare their results. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection of personal information.

2. Information and Permissions

Camera: The app uses your camera to display a live preview, detect body poses, calculate pose-match scores, and capture photographs.

Photos and Images: You can select a reference photo from your device. The app processes the image to detect pose landmarks. Photos and comparison images may be saved to your device's gallery when you choose to save them.

Device and Advertising Information: Our advertising provider, Google AdMob, may collect device identifiers, advertising data, IP address, diagnostics, and ad interaction information as described in its applicable policies and configuration.

App Settings: Your match threshold and preferences, such as auto-capture and live skeleton display, are used to provide the app's functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information and permissions are used to:

  • Detect and compare body poses.
  • Display live match scores and visual guidance.
  • Capture, review, save, and compare photographs.
  • Provide advertising and manage applicable consent choices.
  • Maintain app functionality, security, and stability.

4. On-Device Processing

Pose detection and matching are designed to run on your device. Camera frames are not uploaded to our servers for pose analysis. Reference images are processed for pose detection within the app's workflow.

Third-party SDKs, including advertising services, may independently process information as described in their privacy policies. Actual behavior depends on the app's implementation and SDK configuration.

5. Advertising and Consent

PoseSync AI Camera uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The app may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where applicable.

Depending on your location, consent choices, and configuration, advertising services may use device identifiers and other information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

You can access available privacy and advertising choices through the app's designated controls.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your photographs or upload camera frames for pose analysis.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google AdMob and its associated services, for advertising, diagnostics, and related functions. These providers may process data according to their own policies.

When you use your device's sharing features, the content you choose to share may be sent to the application or recipient you select.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Photographs and comparison images that you save are stored through your device's gallery or supported media-storage mechanisms. The app's local settings and cached data may remain on your device until cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Third-party service providers may retain information according to their applicable policies and settings.

8. Permissions and Your Choices

You can manage camera and other applicable permissions through Android settings. You may choose whether to select reference images, capture photographs, and save or share results.

Disabling permissions may prevent certain features from working.

9. Children's Privacy

PoseSync AI Camera is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and audience settings must be configured in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

If you believe personal information has been collected from a child inappropriately, contact us so we can review the concern.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app, services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
